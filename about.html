Our Story

From Local Supply Depot to Regional Mechanical Giant

Founded in 2001 by mechanical engineers, AQUAPRO was created to solve a recurring contractor headache: supply delays, counterfeit fittings, and inaccurate technical submittals.

Today, our 50,000 sq.ft state-of-the-art central warehouse and testing facility stocks more than 10,000 certified SKUs. We partner directly with global tier-1 manufacturers to supply commercial office skyscrapers, hospital medical suites, luxury residential communities, and municipal water facilities.
