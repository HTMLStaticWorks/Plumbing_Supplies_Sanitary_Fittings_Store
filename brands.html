Strict Verification

Our 5-Stage Manufacturer Vetting Protocol

Not every factory meets AQUAPRO standards. We only onboard manufacturers that satisfy strict metallurgy, hydrostatic bursting pressure, and lead-free non-toxicity audits.

✔️ Stage 1: Chemical metallurgy composition analysis (XRF spectroscopy).
✔️ Stage 2: 1.5x working pressure hydrostatic endurance testing.
✔️ Stage 3: WRAS / NSF 61 potable water health certifications.
✔️ Stage 4: Thermal cycling between 5°C and 95°C for 10,000 cycles.
✔️ Stage 5: Factory ISO 9001:2015 QA management audit.
